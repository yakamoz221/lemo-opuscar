# TREATMENT — "YZ Bülteni: Bir Sorudan Sohbete"

Style: Broadcast News Package (`styles/news-package`). 1080×1920 vertical, 24 fps, ~120 s, Turkish voice (edge-tts `tr-TR-AhmetNeural`), clean frame + separate `.srt`.

## 1. Three structures

A. **Round-up (rundown → items → rundown ticked).** Five dated items, 1950 → 2022, each with its own graphic; the rundown opens and closes the bulletin. Reads as "the history of AI as one evening bulletin".
B. **Countdown.** "5 moments that made AI", from #5 to #1. Punchy, but a countdown ranks, and ranking ChatGPT above Turing is an opinion the facts don't carry.
C. **One lead package + "also today" grid.** Lead: the 2022 ChatGPT launch; a grid of older stories as background. Modern, but it buries the shape of the story (question → winter → games → seeing → talking).

**Chosen: A.** History is a sequence of dated items, and the style's date-in-the-eyebrow and ticked rundown turn 72 years into one programme. The rundown, ticked at the end, is the film's ending: every row a step from a question to a conversation.

Opening image: the sting slams in on black, flies to the corner as the bug, and the band wipes straight into the rundown. Ending: rundown ticked → the last row's stamp → end card with the line "1950 → 2022: Bir sorudan sohbete".

## 2. Logline and arc

A news bulletin from the future's archive: in five dated stories, machines go from a question on paper (1950) to a conversation with everyone (2022).
Setup: the question (Turing, Dartmouth). Turn: the winter (Lighthill), then the machines start winning (chess, Go) and seeing (ImageNet crosses the human line). Ending: they talk (Transformer → GPT-3 → ChatGPT).

## 3. Benchmark

Learn: evening-news headline menus (a rundown that returns ticked), financial-channel split-flap boards, a threshold line that breaks. Don't learn: any broadcaster's logo, set, colours or ident; no anchor; no crawl.

## 6. Cue map

120 BPM, minor key. Sting: tutti hit. Rundown: theme full, glockenspiel per row. Items 1–2: pizzicato + horn pads (policy mood), timpani on story changes. Item 3: violins down, heartbeat kick. Item 4: pulse; **one beat of silence** before the 2015 step, then the heaviest hit, held drone under the stamp. Item 5: major-key lift. Recap: theme, click + glock per tick. End card: tutti.
